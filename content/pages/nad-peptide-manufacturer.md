## How Does NAD+ Work

### Redox Cycling and Energy Metabolism

NAD+ cycles between NAD+ and NADH, accepting electrons in glycolysis, TCA cycle, and beta-oxidation.

### Sirtuin-Mediated Deacetylation

NAD+ is an obligate co-substrate for sirtuins, linking cellular NAD+ levels to gene expression.

### PARP Activation and DNA Repair

PARPs consume NAD+ for PARylation in DNA damage response.

### CD38 and Calcium Signaling

CD38 consumes NAD+ to generate cADPR, a calcium-mobilizing second messenger.

### Molecular Pathway Summary

| Pathway | NAD+ Role | Downstream Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glycolysis/TCA | Electron acceptor (NAD+ -> NADH) | ATP production |
| Sirtuins (SIRT1-7) | Co-substrate | Gene regulation, mitophagy |
| PARP1 | Substrate for PARylation | DNA repair |
| CD38 | Substrate for cADPR | Calcium mobilization |
| OxPhos | Electron donor (NADH -> NAD+) | ATP synthesis |
