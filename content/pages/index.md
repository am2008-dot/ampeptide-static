## AMP Peptide Story: 10 Years of Peptide Manufacturing Experience

AMP Peptide is a China-based peptide manufacturer focused on custom peptide synthesis, research peptides, cosmetic peptides, and bulk peptide supply.

Since 2012, we have worked with research laboratories, biotechnology companies, and ingredient buyers from different countries. Some customers come to us with a peptide sequence, while others simply ask whether a certain project can be produced.

## AMP PEPTIDE MANUFACTURER

AMP Peptide Co., Ltd. is located in Suzhou Industrial Park, Jiangsu, China. Our facility covers about 3,000 square meters, including a controlled GMP workshop area for higher-grade production needs.

Our work is built around solid-phase peptide synthesis, purification, lyophilization, and analytical testing. Over the years, we have handled small research-scale orders, custom modified peptides, cosmetic peptide ingredients, and bulk peptide projects.

We know peptide projects can fail for small reasons: poor solubility, wrong counter ion, unstable sequence, weak purification method, or unclear storage conditions. That is why AMP Peptide reviews the technical details before production starts.
