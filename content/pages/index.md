## AMP Peptide Story: 10 Years of Peptide Manufacturing Experience

AMP Peptide is a China-based peptide manufacturer focused on custom peptide synthesis, research peptides, cosmetic peptides, and bulk peptide supply.

Since 2012, we have worked with research laboratories, biotechnology companies, and ingredient buyers from different countries. Some customers come to us with a peptide sequence, while others simply ask whether a certain project can be produced.

Our job is straightforward. We review the requirements, discuss the production details, and provide practical support from small-scale research quantities to larger manufacturing projects.

---

## AMP PEPTIDE MANUFACTURER

AMP Peptide Co., Ltd. is located in Suzhou Industrial Park, Jiangsu, China. Our facility covers about 3,000 square meters, including a controlled GMP workshop area for higher-grade production needs.

Our work is built around solid-phase peptide synthesis, purification, lyophilization, and analytical testing. Over the years, we have handled small research-scale orders, custom modified peptides, cosmetic peptide ingredients, and bulk peptide projects.

We know peptide projects can fail for small reasons: poor solubility, wrong counter ion, unstable sequence, weak purification method, or unclear storage conditions. That is why AMP Peptide reviews the technical details before production starts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does AMP Peptide do?

AMP Peptide is a China-based peptide manufacturer specializing in custom peptide synthesis, research peptides, cosmetic peptides, and bulk peptide manufacturing.

### Where is AMP Peptide located?

AMP Peptide is located in Suzhou Industrial Park, Jiangsu, China, one of China's leading biotechnology and pharmaceutical manufacturing centers.

### How much experience does AMP Peptide have?

The founding team behind AMP Peptide has more than 14 years of experience in peptide synthesis, purification, analytical testing, and scale-up manufacturing.

### Does AMP Peptide provide custom peptide synthesis?

Yes. AMP Peptide provides custom peptide synthesis services based on customer sequences, purity requirements, and modification specifications.

### What types of peptides does AMP Peptide manufacture?

AMP Peptide manufactures research peptides, cosmetic peptides, GLP-1 analog peptides, modified peptides, long peptides, cyclic peptides, PEGylated peptides, and biotinylated peptides.

### Which peptide modifications are supported?

AMP Peptide supports PEGylation, biotinylation, fluorescent labeling, lipidation, cyclic peptide synthesis, and incorporation of non-natural amino acids.

### What quantity ranges can AMP Peptide supply?

AMP Peptide supports projects ranging from milligram-scale research quantities to kilogram-scale manufacturing.

### What purity levels are available?

Typical purity levels include 95%, 98%, 99%, and 99.5%, depending on project requirements.

### Does AMP Peptide provide analytical reports?

Yes. Analytical reports may include HPLC chromatograms, LC-MS data, Certificate of Analysis (COA), residual solvent analysis, and water content results.
