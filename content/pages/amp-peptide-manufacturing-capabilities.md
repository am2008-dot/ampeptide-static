# AMP Peptide Manufacturing Capabilities

## Introduction

AMP Peptide operates an integrated peptide manufacturing platform in Suzhou, China. Our facility combines peptide synthesis, purification, lyophilization, analytical testing, and process development to support projects ranging from milligram-scale research quantities to larger manufacturing requirements.

Over the years, AMP Peptide has continued to invest in equipment, laboratory infrastructure, and technical expertise to provide reliable support for custom peptide synthesis, modified peptides, cosmetic peptides, and bulk peptide production.

---

## Our Manufacturing Facility

AMP Peptide is located in Suzhou, one of China’s major biotechnology centers. The facility occupies approximately 5,000 square meters and includes dedicated areas for peptide synthesis, purification, analytical testing, storage, and research activities.

The manufacturing site was designed with expansion in mind, allowing additional cleanroom and production capacity to be added as demand increases.

### Facility Highlights

- Approximately 5,000 m² of operational space
- Dedicated synthesis and purification areas
- Analytical quality control laboratories

## AMP Peptide Manufacturing Capabilities – FAQ

### What is the core manufacturing capability of AMP Peptide?

AMP Peptide operates an integrated peptide manufacturing platform that includes solid-phase peptide synthesis (SPPS), purification, lyophilization, analytical testing, and process development.

---

### What peptide synthesis technology does AMP Peptide use?

AMP Peptide primarily uses solid-phase peptide synthesis (SPPS) technology, which allows stepwise assembly of amino acid sequences with controlled coupling, deprotection, and purification cycles.

---

### What types of peptides can be synthesized?

AMP Peptide can synthesize a wide range of peptides, including linear peptides, cyclic peptides, long-chain peptides, modified peptides, GLP-1 analogs, and aggregation-prone sequences.

---

### What synthesis equipment is used?

AMP Peptide operates automated peptide synthesizers, including microwave-assisted synthesis systems, which improve coupling efficiency, reduce synthesis time, and enhance reproducibility.

---

### Does AMP Peptide support scale-up manufacturing?

Yes. AMP Peptide supports scale-up from milligram research quantities to gram-scale and kilogram-scale production, depending on project requirements and process validation.

---

### What purification methods are used?

Peptide purification is performed using preparative High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC), optimized according to peptide sequence, hydrophobicity, length, and modification type.

---

### What purity levels can be achieved?

Typical purity targets include:

- ≥95% for research peptides
- ≥98% for advanced research applications
- ≥99% for high-purity projects
- Up to ≥99.5% for specialized requirements

---

### What analytical methods are used for quality control?

AMP Peptide uses multiple analytical techniques, including HPLC for purity analysis, LC-MS for molecular weight confirmation, and additional testing such as residual solvent analysis and water content measurement when required.

---

### Does AMP Peptide provide LC-MS confirmation?

Yes. LC-MS is used to confirm peptide identity by verifying molecular weight and detecting possible truncated or modified impurities.

---

### What is included in batch release testing?

Each batch undergoes internal QC review, including HPLC analysis, LC-MS confirmation, specification checks, and final approval before release.

---

### Does AMP Peptide offer difficult sequence synthesis?

Yes. AMP Peptide has experience with challenging peptides, including hydrophobic sequences, long peptides, aggregation-prone peptides, multi-disulfide peptides, and cyclic structures.

---

### What is lyophilization used for in peptide manufacturing?

Lyophilization (freeze-drying) is used to convert purified peptide solutions into stable powder forms, improving stability, storage life, and transport efficiency.

---

### Does AMP Peptide provide batch traceability?

Yes. Batch traceability includes raw material records, synthesis documentation, analytical reports, batch numbers, and storage information for full process tracking.

---

### What documents are provided with each batch?

Each batch may include a Certificate of Analysis (COA), HPLC chromatograms, LC-MS data, and additional analytical reports depending on project requirements.

---

### What is the typical manufacturing workflow?

The typical workflow includes:  
sequence review → raw material preparation → peptide synthesis → cleavage → purification → lyophilization → analytical testing → batch release → packaging and shipment.

---

### What scale of production is supported?

AMP Peptide supports production from milligram-scale research quantities up to gram-level and kilogram-scale manufacturing projects, depending on sequence complexity and process feasibility.

---

### What makes AMP Peptide’s manufacturing platform different?

AMP Peptide integrates synthesis, purification, analytical testing, and process development under one system, enabling consistent quality control, scale-up capability, and technical communication across all project stages.

---

### Does AMP Peptide support global supply?

Yes. AMP Peptide supplies customers in more than 30 countries and provides export documentation and international shipping support.
