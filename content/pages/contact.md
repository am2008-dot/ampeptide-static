## Custom Peptide & Research Supply Support

AMP Peptide supports laboratories, distributors, and research organizations with custom peptide synthesis, wholesale research peptide supply, quality documentation, and international shipping coordination.

- Request quotations for milligram to bulk-scale peptide projects.
- Ask for available quality documents such as COA, HPLC, and MS references.
- Confirm shipping options, packaging requirements, and research-use documentation.

## What to Include in Your Inquiry

To help the team review your request efficiently, include the peptide name or sequence, target purity, quantity range, preferred modifications, and any documentation requirements. For custom synthesis projects, details such as terminal modifications, labeling, solubility requirements, salt form, and analytical expectations can help clarify the quotation scope.

## Quality and Documentation Requests

Customers may ask about available quality references, including HPLC purity data, mass spectrometry confirmation, COA documentation, batch information, and packaging details. AMP Peptide can also discuss research-use labeling, storage guidance, and logistics documentation for wholesale or laboratory supply workflows.

## Research-Use Communication Notice

AMP Peptide products and related communications are intended for qualified research, laboratory, or manufacturing evaluation contexts. The team does not provide medical advice, treatment guidance, or personal-use recommendations. Contact the team for product availability, synthesis feasibility, documentation, and shipping support.

## Contact FAQ

### What information helps with a custom synthesis quote?

Please provide the peptide sequence, target quantity, purity grade, modification requirements, preferred analytical documentation, and intended research or evaluation context. If the sequence is confidential, note that in the inquiry so the team can suggest an appropriate communication workflow.

### Can AMP Peptide support wholesale or distributor requests?

Yes. Wholesale inquiries can include expected order volume, product list, destination country, packaging preferences, labeling needs, and recurring supply requirements. This helps the team evaluate production planning, available inventory, shipping routes, and documentation support.

### What type of support is not provided?

AMP Peptide does not provide medical consultation, treatment instructions, human-use recommendations, or personal dosing guidance. Communications are limited to product availability, research-use supply, manufacturing feasibility, quality documentation, logistics, and commercial support.

For best results, use the contact form or listed business communication channel and include clear project details. The AMP Peptide team can then route the inquiry to sales, production, quality, or logistics support according to the request type.
