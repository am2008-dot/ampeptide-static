# About AMP Peptide

AMP Peptide – Advanced Molecular Products Peptide Manufacturer

## Introduction

AMP Peptide is a peptide manufacturer based in Suzhou, China, focused on custom peptide synthesis, research peptides, cosmetic peptides, and bulk peptide manufacturing.

Since the company was established, AMP Peptide has continued to invest in facilities, analytical capabilities, and technical talent to support peptide projects ranging from early-stage research to larger-scale production.

Today, AMP Peptide serves customers from biotechnology companies, research laboratories, pharmaceutical development organizations, and ingredient manufacturers in multiple countries.

## Our Beginning

### ***From an Idea to a Peptide Manufacturing Company***

AMP Peptide was founded in 2012 with the goal of building a modern peptide manufacturing platform in Suzhou, one of China’s leading biotechnology centers.

From the beginning, the company focused on combining peptide synthesis, purification, analytical testing, and process development under one roof. Continuous investment in facilities and equipment has allowed us to expand its manufacturing capabilities and support customers in different stages of product development.

By 2026, we had established partnerships with customers in more than 30 countries and continued to expand its global presence.
